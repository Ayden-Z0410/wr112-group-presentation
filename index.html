“Navigating Modes of Communication:
Experiences of Being Bilingual”

Sue Yuan

Ayden · Ian · Mufan

Opening: Personal Narrative

Discussion Question

Why does Yuan begin her argumentative essay with a personal story?

Selected Passage

Discussion Question

Yuan explains that bilingual people may respond differently to the same story depending on the language in which they hear it. What might this suggest about the connection between language, memory, and emotion?

Code-Switching and Identity

Discussion Question

Yuan argues that code-switching can reflect a speaker’s ‘moral alignments and changing social identities.’ Do you think switching between languages can actually change how a person presents themselves, or does it simply change how they express an identity they already have? Why?

Connection to Boroditsky Lera Boroditsky, “How Does Our Language Shape the Way We Think?”

Discussion Question

Both Sue Yuan and Lera Boroditsky argue that language can influence the way people experience the world. However, Yuan focuses more on cultural identity and social behavior, while Boroditsky focuses on cognitive processes. What does this difference tell us about the relationship between language, culture, and thought?
